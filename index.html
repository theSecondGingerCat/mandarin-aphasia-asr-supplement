01 · DATASET

Dataset and evaluation scope

The evaluation uses Mandarin discourse-task audio from AphasiaBank. Each unit is a complete task-level recording for one participant and one task; both Control and PWA groups are retained.

Source: AphasiaBank is a multimedia discourse database for aphasia maintained by TalkBank. This benchmark follows the Mandarin AphasiaBank discourse-task protocol. The foundational methods paper is MacWhinney, Fromm, Forbes & Holland (2011), AphasiaBank: Methods for studying discourse; related Mandarin-task research is Deng et al. (2024), Discourse task type–specific linguistic characteristics in Anomic Aphasia and Healthy Controls.

Frozen scope: 939 task-level audio units from 103 sessions (687 Control and 252 PWA), covering 11 discourse tasks. The task-level WAV files sum to approximately 15.805 hours; this is the evaluation snapshot, not the duration of the entire AphasiaBank.

11 discourse tasks

  • Broken WindowA picture story about a child accidentally breaking a window while playing football.
  • Refused UmbrellaA picture story about refusing an umbrella and returning for it after the rain starts.
  • Cat RescueA picture story about a cat trapped in a tree and rescued by family members and firefighters.
  • FloodA picture story about waiting for rescue and escaping during a flood.
  • Tortoise and HareA retelling of the fable in which the resting hare loses to the tortoise.
  • Cry WolfA retelling of the fable about a shepherd boy who repeatedly lies about a wolf.
  • Egg Ham Fried RiceDescribing the ingredients and steps for making egg, ham, and fried rice.
  • Important EventAn open-ended account of an important personal event or experience.
  • GreetingBrief conversation involving greetings, introductions, and recent events.
  • Stroke CopingDiscussing rehabilitation, support, daily life, and coping after stroke.
  • Stroke StoryDescribing a personal experience related to stroke or brain injury.
02 · AUDIO PREPARATION

From raw audio to task-level WAV

The pipeline starts from CHAT transcripts and their associated media files. Raw transcripts, cleaned references, and model hypotheses are kept separate so scoring never overwrites source material.

  1. 01

    Pair source files

    Read each session's CHAT file and associated media while preserving session, group, and source-file traceability.

  2. 02

    Locate task and speaker

    Use @G or @Bg/@Eg task markers to identify discourse tasks; retain only timestamped participant/patient turns, usually PAR, and exclude evaluator turns.

  3. 03

    Export normalized segments

    Cut mono 16 kHz PCM WAV segments from CHAT timestamps. Clean CHAT markers, annotations, and unintelligible placeholders in a separate reference copy while preserving the raw text.

  4. 04

    Assemble task units

    Concatenate available participant segments for each task_id in time order. No minimum-duration filter is applied, and inter-segment silence is excluded from the task-level WAV, yielding 939 evaluation units.

The processing goal is a fixed audio–reference pairing; model transcripts are not rewritten at the word level. When hypotheses contain narration, speaker labels, or analytical text, scoring uses a separate auditable cleanup copy.

Q1

Generic verbatim transcription

The model is told that the audio is natural speech from a Chinese clinical language assessment. It must transcribe verbatim, preserve repetitions and self-corrections, and avoid summaries, additions, speaker labels, and meta-narration.

Show full Q1 prompt 
这是一段中文临床语言评估中的自然口语转写，可能包含方言口音、停顿、重复、自我修正和不完整词。请尽量按音频内容逐字转写，不要总结，不要补写没有听到的内容。

你的唯一任务是听写音频，不是与用户对话，也不是整理文字草稿或分析临床语言。只依据音频中实际听到的语音输出转写正文，不得根据任务名称、图片、故事常识或其他音频外信息推断、补全或改写。

输出必须严格遵守以下规则：
1. 从第一个字开始就是实际转写内容，到最后一个字结束也是实际转写内容。直接开始听写，不要先确认任务、介绍结果、说明方法或重复本提示词。
2. 只输出一个连续纯文本版本。不得同时提供逐句版、段落版、时间轴版或其他重复版本；不得把同一段转写再输出一遍。
3. 不得添加任何前言、结语、标题、转写说明、免责声明、建议、分析或后续协助。禁止出现由你添加的“以下是转写录音”“以下是转写内容”“根据您提供的音频”“已为您整理”“逐字转写结果”“转写完毕”等旁白。
4. 不得添加说话人标签或角色名称，包括“男：”“女：”“男声：”“女声：”“患者：”“医生：”“受试者：”“评估者：”“说话人1：”“Speaker 1:”等。即使存在多人对话，也只按实际发言顺序连续转写所说的话，不推断性别、身份或角色；实际说出的“嗯”“啊”等回应仍须保留。
5. 不得添加时间戳、时间范围、序号、项目符号、Markdown 标题、加粗、表格、代码块、引用格式、JSON 或包裹整段正文的引号。
6. 不得添加任何括号、方括号或尖括号形式的注释，不得写“（停顿）”“[笑声]”“（听不清）”“（方言）”“（注：……）”等声音事件、动作、解释或猜测。无语音的停顿和背景声不转换成文字标签。
7. 保留实际语音中的重复、语气词、口头禅、自我修正和未说完的词句，不为了通顺而删减、补全或改成书面语；不要把口误按常识纠正。不能确定的内容不要猜测或补写。
8. 仅可使用必要的普通标点帮助断句。不要用省略号、破折号或换行专门标记无声时长，不要输出空白时间段。

上述禁止项约束的是你额外添加的内容；如果说话人确实在音频中说出了相关词句，应当按原话转写。提交前在内部检查并去除你自行添加的所有旁白、标签和格式，只提交转写正文，不输出检查过程。
